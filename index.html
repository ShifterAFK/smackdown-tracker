About this tier list

S: top 5 with 70% or more. A: 60% or more. B: 50% or more. C: below 50%. At least 10 recorded appearances are required, otherwise a hero is listed as preliminary. Click a portrait for details. Banned heroes are shown in grayscale.

An appearance means one hero on one team in an approved match. Win rate is the share of those team appearances that ended in a win. Overall combines all maps in this event, not other game modes. Win rate describes team results, not the strength of a hero on its own.

Conservative score is the lower bound of a 95% Wilson interval. It is not a power rating. Repeated players, selection bias and team composition can affect results. A duo win rate is the win rate of teams containing that pair, whatever the third hero or Phantimal was, so it does not measure synergy on its own. Minimum appearances only filters the table, it does not guarantee reliability.

Mirrors are excluded separately for each category and the approved match count is not reduced. Statistics support map filters, but not hero by Phantimal breakdowns.

How to use

This tracker collects real matches from the current event and turns them into community statistics. It works the same on a phone and on a computer.

  1. Check the map.

    The map shown follows the current rotation. If your match was played on a different one, tap Change map and choose it. Tap the preview to enlarge it.

  2. Add the heroes.

    Fill the six slots in draft order: BLUE 1, RED 1, RED 2, BLUE 2, BLUE 3, RED 3. Tap a slot and then a hero, or drag a portrait into a slot. Each hero can appear only once per match. Use × on a slot to remove a hero, or drag one slot onto another to swap them.

  3. Set the Phantimals.

    When the draft order is known, the Phantimal is set automatically from the first hero who is not Dimensional. If you do not know the order, untick Draft order known and choose the Phantimal you saw in battle, or leave it as Unknown.

  4. Send the result.

    Choose the winning side, complete the verification and press Submit match once. Wait for the success message. If the request times out, leave the match as it is and retry without changing the match to reuse its submission ID.

Draft Assistant

The assistant suggests heroes, it never picks for you. Open it with the Assistant button, choose your side and record the draft as it happens. Suggestions for your side update after every pick, including the very first pick of the other team.

Suggestions are ranked from Overall recorded results: hero results, results with heroes already on your side, and results against heroes already picked by the other side. A short note such as counters a hero appears only when enough matches support it.

Tap a suggestion to add it, or drag it into a slot. Use × to hide a hero you do not own, Restore brings hidden heroes back and Reset teams starts a new draft. Suggestions always use all maps together. Changing the match map does not change suggestion data. Arena positioning is not evaluated.

The assistant does not know which heroes you own, so check your own roster. Results describe teams, not single heroes, and heroes with few recorded matches are ranked more carefully. Press Close Assistant when you are done.

Tier list and statistics

S is the top five heroes with at least 70% wins and 10 appearances. A is 60% or more, B is 50% or more and C is below 50%. Heroes with fewer than 10 appearances are listed as preliminary.

In Statistics, choose a map, a category, a minimum number of appearances and a sort order. The default sort is the conservative score, so a hero with only a few matches does not jump to the top.

Numbers are cached for up to five minutes, so a fresh submission can take a moment to appear. Check the calculation time shown above the table.
