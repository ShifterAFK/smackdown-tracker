## Recaptured primary-source sections

### Talene — tracker ID: `talene`
Source: https://afkstargazer.com/en/skill/talene

- **Exclusive Equipment — Pyre of Renewal:** A persistent effect is assigned to the frontmost ally other than Talene. The carrier damages adjacent enemies and heals from damage dealt; healing received builds up to three permanent damage increases. At maximum stacks, the carrier can resurrect once after defeat.
- **Rivalry Mode:** Casting an Ultimate increases damage dealt for 8 seconds. The first Ultimate also permanently improves Energy recovery from attacks. Refine 4 adds Vitality and Healing.

### Cecia — tracker ID: `cecia`
Source: https://afkstargazer.com/en/skill/cecia

- **Earth's Offering:** Increases Cecia's and Mr. Carlyle's ATK SPD for 8 seconds. Later levels improve the bonus; one level replaces Carlyle's next normal attack with a 2-tile area quake and grants Life Drain.
- **Agonizing Puncture:** Cecia and Mr. Carlyle gain DEF Penetration. After three normal attacks, Cecia's next normal attack is enhanced; later levels increase the penetration and enhanced-attack damage.
- **Hero Focus:** Increases Cecia's ATK SPD and grants an additional bonus while Mr. Carlyle is on the battlefield.

### Granny Dahnie — tracker ID: `granny-dahnie`
Source: https://afkstargazer.com/en/skill/granny-dahnie

- **Enhance Force:** Seed Cannon restores HP when it fires. The next level also restores Energy, grants temporary Haste and strengthens the seed's ATK reduction.
- **Season Charm:** Deals recurring magic damage to adjacent enemies and heals based on actual damage dealt. Higher ranks improve damage/healing and add an opening unaffected window.

### Valka — tracker ID: `valka`
Source: https://afkstargazer.com/en/skill/valka

- **Enhance Force:** While Spectral Bulwark's shield is active, Valka gains extra Energy from normal attacks. The next level scales Ultimate damage with ATK SPD and Haste and grants a Bulwark-style shield after Ultimate.
- **Season Charm:** Increases damage dealt and reduces damage taken; both bonuses are stronger on the enemy side. Higher ranks add a permanent shield on first entry to the enemy side.

### Indris — tracker ID: `indris`
Source: https://afkstargazer.com/en/skill/indris

- **Hero Focus:** Increases ATK during battle, with an additional bonus while any enemy has exposed weakness.
- **Exclusive Equipment — Windpiercer:** Triggering exposed-weakness bonus damage increases ATK SPD for 6 seconds. Higher ranks improve the speed bonus and add a Phys DEF reduction to normal attacks.
- **Rivalry Mode:** Normal attacks stack ATK SPD and normal-attack damage, up to 10 stacks. Refine 4 adds ATK SPD and Execution.

### Reinier — tracker ID: `reinier`
Source: https://afkstargazer.com/en/skill/reinier

- **Enhance Force:** Increases the allied hero's ATK by 4% until battle end when that ally occupies a symmetrical position to an enemy.
- **Season Charm:** At battle start, increases ranged damage for the ally one tile behind Reinier. Higher ranks add a one-time shield when that ally first drops below 50% HP.

### Satrana — tracker ID: `satrana`
Source: https://afkstargazer.com/en/skill/satrana

- **Rivalry Mode:** Casting an Ultimate increases damage dealt for 8 seconds; the first Ultimate also permanently improves Energy recovery from attacks. Refine 4 adds Haste and Ranged DEF.
- **Enhance Force:** Vixen Rush's cooldown is removed when all enemy heroes are ignited or five enemies in total have been ignited. The next level improves ignite damage and lowers the trigger threshold.
- **Season Charm:** Each enemy affected by continuous damage increases ATK up to a cap. Higher ranks also reduce the target's Phys and Magic DEF.

### Nerion — tracker ID: `nerion`
Source: https://afkstargazer.com/en/skill/nerion

- **Tidal Rebuke:** After every three normal attacks, the next normal attack is enhanced; it knocks the target back, deals damage and stuns. Later levels increase the damage.
- **Riptide Wrath:** Throws a spear at the area with the most enemies, damaging nearby enemies; two seconds later a current erupts, launching enemies and dealing a second hit. Later levels increase both damage values.

### Mehira — tracker ID: `mehira`
Source: https://afkstargazer.com/en/skill/mehira

- **Season Charm:** Restores Energy per second, with the amount halved after 10 seconds. After three Ultimate casts, increases ATK; higher ranks add true damage to all enemies whenever the caster uses an Ultimate.

### Bryon — tracker ID: `bryon`
Source: https://afkstargazer.com/en/skill/bryon

- **Enhance Force:** When Elona is present as Leafblade Gale is cast, Bryon generates two leaves beside her. The next level lets thunderbolts jump to two enemies and permanently increases Bryon's Haste after repeated Elona hits.
- **Season Charm:** Increases ATK SPD and restores HP based on normal-attack damage. Higher ranks add Penetration while the caster remains above 50% HP.
