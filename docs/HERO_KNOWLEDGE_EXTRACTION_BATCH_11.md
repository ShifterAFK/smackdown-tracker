### Bryon — tracker ID: `bryon`

Source: https://afkstargazer.com/en/skill/bryon

**Page tags:** `Cheat Death (Self)`, `Debuff (AOE)`, `Energy Denial`, `Summon`, `Ult (Opening)`.

- **Ultimate — Falcon Raid:** `Debuff (AOE)`, `Summon`, `Ult (Opening)`; opening 1000 Energy and summons Elona. Active spends Energy to call Elona to attack current target; Elona remains on field until Energy is depleted or Bryon is defeated. Later levels improve Elona's attack, Haste and thunderbolt effects.
- **Leafblade Gale:** summons four magic leaves to attack target.
- **Shadow Flash:** `Energy Denial`; passive: normal attacks and skills hit twice and absorb Energy. Active deals two hits.
- **Hero Focus:** Haste; extra Haste while Elona is present.
- **Exclusive Equipment — Tacit Strike:** `Cheat Death (Self)`; Elona counterattacks and stuns when Bryon is controlled or takes a sufficiently large hit. LV 2: first fatal blow is blocked by Elona consuming Energy; Bryon heals and becomes untargetable temporarily.
- **Rivalry Mode:** separate normal-attack stacking and refine stats.
- **Capture excerpt ends before Enhance Force and Season Charm; recapture remainder.**

### Estrilda — tracker ID: `estrilda`

Source: https://afkstargazer.com/en/skill/estrilda

**Page tags:** `Airborne`, `Debuff (Single Target)`, `Temp Buff (AOE)`.

- **Ultimate — Piercing Onslaught:** three charges along different straight paths, damaging and stunning enemies; final charge crosses battlefield and ends with Ride Down.
- **Gallant Charge:** `Airborne`; opening Ride Down charges through enemies, pushing the first target struck; target pushed to edge is knocked airborne and down. Active cast sweeps lance against adjacent enemies.
- **Lance Flurry:** `Airborne`, `Debuff (Single Target)`; rapid lance strikes reduce target ATK for 12 s; damage increases when Estrilda is at enemy edge.
- **Hero Focus:** ATK.
- **Exclusive Equipment — Pennon of Valor:** `Debuff (Single Target)`, `Temp Buff (AOE)`; when Ride Down reaches enemy edge, plants a pennon that buffs allied ATK and reduces damage taken. Higher rank marks pushed enemy to take increased damage.
- **Enhance Force:** after Piercing Onslaught, heals and shields Estrilda; after Ride Down, grants Penetration and ATK.
- **Season Charm:** own-skill displacement grants ATK; higher ranks also grant a shield.
- **Rivalry Mode:** separate opening/assist/defeat stats and refine effects.

### Kordan — tracker ID: `kordan`

Source: https://afkstargazer.com/en/skill/kordan

**Page tags:** `Airborne`, `Debuff (AOE)`, `Heal Denial`, `Temp Buff (AOE)`, `Ult (Opening)`.

- **Ultimate — Dominance Ring:** `Heal Denial`, `Temp Buff (AOE)`, `Ult (Opening)`; begins with 1000 Energy. Challenges one adjacent enemy and immobilizes them; creates a hunting circle around Kordan and target. Units inside take reduced damage/healing from outside sources; allies inside gain ATK, Life Drain and damage dealt.
- **Fury Slash:** sweeping slash damages enemy and grants Kordan shield based on damage dealt plus ATK.
- **Sundering Strike:** leaps and slams enemy, dealing damage and knocking them down.
- **Hero Focus:** ATK.
- **Exclusive Equipment — Rage Unleashed:** `Airborne`; after first assist/defeat in hunting circle or after 20 s fighting inside it, Fury Slash and Sundering Strike are enhanced. Later levels grant permanent DEF stacks and healing after assists/defeats.
- **Enhance Force:** assists/defeats inside hunting circle make Kordan leap to a new target and reposition circle; extends duration and grants allies inside Penetration.
- **Season Charm:** `Debuff (AOE)`; damage taken reduced per adjacent enemy; higher ranks periodically damage nearby enemies and reduce their ATK.
- **Rivalry Mode:** separate timed defensive/offensive effect and refine stats.

### Sonja — tracker ID: `sonja`

Source: https://afkstargazer.com/en/skill/sonja

**Page tags:** `Temp Buff (AOE)`.

- **Ultimate — Tempest Thrusts:** eight piercing strikes in frontal area, each with current-HP-based extra damage, followed by a charge; control-immune while casting.
- **Crimson Covenant:** opening link with two allies to her left and right, increasing their ATK and Phys/Magic DEF. Bonuses grow over time. If a buffed ally is defeated, covenant ends and Sonja absorbs accumulated stats.
- **Stunning Reception:** spins and slashes nearby enemies twice, stunning them.
- **Hero Focus:** Haste; extra Haste after first Ultimate.
- **Exclusive Equipment — Unbreakable Bond:** grants Initial Energy and improves Crimson Covenant so Sonja benefits from accumulated bonuses while buffed allies remain alive. Higher levels share damage reduction among covenant members.
- **Enhance Force:** converts part of Tempest Thrusts damage into self-healing.
- **Season Charm:** `Temp Buff (AOE)`; every 8 s increases ATK and Life Drain for nearby allies; higher ranks add a shield for Sonja per ally affected.
- **Rivalry Mode:** separate timed defensive/offensive effect and refine stats.

### Vala — tracker ID: `vala`

Source: https://afkstargazer.com/en/skill/vala

**Page tags:** `Debuff (Single Target)`, `Energy Denial`, `Special Target`.

- **Ultimate — Swift Shift:** switches between Skyblaster Mode (shooting/stunning marked enemy at range) and Sword Mode (rushes marked enemy for true damage and converts normal attacks to melee). Auto mode chooses based on target HP ratio.
- **Notice Beforehand:** `Energy Denial`, `Special Target`; marks farthest enemy at battle start, prioritizes them and absorbs Energy per hit. Retargets farthest enemy in Skyblaster Mode or nearest in Sword Mode.
- **Checkmate:** `Debuff (Single Target)`; in Skyblaster Mode shoots anesthetic bullet and reduces Haste; in Sword Mode hits three times and stuns.
- **Hero Focus:** ATK; extra ATK for each non-summoned enemy defeated.
- **Exclusive Equipment — Night Maneuver:** when marked enemy is defeated, Vala becomes untargetable until all other allies are defeated, gaining movement speed and Haste.
- **Enhance Force:** `Debuff (Single Target)`; increases damage to marked enemy. LV 2 marked enemies deal less damage; Skyblaster gains Energy on attack, Sword gains Execution.
- **Season Charm:** opening damage and Haste; higher ranks restore HP and extend effect after assists/defeats.
- **Rivalry Mode:** separate opening/assist/defeat stats and refine effects.
- **Targeting:** page documents furthest-enemy selection and hex-ID tie-breaks; retain as structured targeting metadata.

### Isabella — tracker ID: `isabella`

Source: https://afkstargazer.com/en/skill/isabella

**Per-skill tags visible:** `Haste (Single Target)`, `Special Target`, `Temp Buff (Single Target)` on Ultimate; `Debuff (Single Target)` on Hexward and Exclusive Equipment.

- **Ultimate — Grimoire Pact:** `Haste (Single Target)`, `Special Target`, `Temp Buff (Single Target)`; at battle start selects frontmost ally as companion. Stat boosts received by companion create permanent spell-note stacks. Ultimate buffs companion ATK/Haste and adds corresponding stats from stored notes.
- **Lingering Grace:** heals companion and damages enemies adjacent to companion; part of damage becomes additional healing.

- **Enhance Force:** qualifying stat buffs received by companion grant extra spell-note stack. LV 2 adds Energy per note and increases each note's stat bonus; if allied Silvina is deployed, she is treated as an additional companion.
- **Season Charm:** opening melee-damage buff and ranged damage reduction for ally one tile in front; higher ranks add Energy on Hit.
- **Rivalry Mode:** separate first-low-HP ally heal/shield and refine stats.

### Frieren — tracker ID: `frieren`

Source: https://afkstargazer.com/en/skill/frieren

**Page tags:** `Airborne`, `Debuff (AOE)`, `Special Target`.

- **Ultimate — Zoltraak:** moves to rearmost position maximizing number of enemies hit; strikes a 3-tile rectangle for 3 s, dealing damage per second and splitting large true damage among enemies hit.
- **Lightning: Judradjim:** opening concealment makes enemies less likely to target Frieren. At 15 s, amplifies magic power, increases damage, replaces normal attack and changes target priority. Fighting with Himmel for 5 s immediately triggers amplification and grants permanent ATK based on Himmel's initial ATK.
- **Hellfire: Vollzanbel:** `Debuff (AOE)`; damages target and adjacent enemies, burns area for 5 s, causing HP loss and Vitality reduction. Cooldown halved after magic amplification.
- **Hero Focus:** ATK; extra ATK after magic amplification.
- **Exclusive Equipment — Defensive Magic:** `Special Target`; opening defensive magic protects Frieren and frontmost ally, reducing excess damage above a max-HP threshold.
- **Enhance Force:** `Airborne`; 5 s after magic amplification, knocks highest-cumulative-damage enemy airborne and hurls them toward battlefield edge.
- **Season Charm:** Energy recovery per second; after three Ultimates adds ATK, with higher ranks adding true damage to all enemies.
- **Rivalry Mode:** separate Ultimate-triggered damage/Energy and refine stats.
- **Targeting:** page specifies frontmost ally scan order by row and hex ID; preserve separately.
